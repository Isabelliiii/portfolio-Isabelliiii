PRODUCT URBAN PLANNING GIS

Tree Planting Decision System

A data-driven tool for optimising urban tree placement to maximise environmental and community benefit across Philadelphia neighbourhoods.

Frontend

The public-facing map interface lets residents and planners explore candidate planting sites filtered by heat exposure, canopy gap, and community priority scores.

Visit Frontend  ↗

Backend

The backend pipeline processes spatial and environmental datasets to score and rank candidate sites, powering the prioritisation logic behind the map.
